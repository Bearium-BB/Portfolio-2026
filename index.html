About Me

Game Dev.
Game Enjoyer.

I'm a game developer from Winnipeg, and I've been working in games for about five years. I spent a year as a Unity 3D developer at Illumia Labs, building API systems that connect VR and PC apps to large language models, multiplayer networking for VR, and a multiplayer gallery for an aviation college that ran on both PC and WebGL. I'm now finishing the Game Development Programming advanced diploma at Red River Polytechnic.

As a player, I love RTS and MOBA games, and I'm drawn to the systems that make them work. I also enjoy hosting game servers and experimenting with server hardware, which feeds into the way I think about networking and architecture.

Outside of work and school, I'm always building random side projects just to see how far an idea can go. Most of them end up in the Laboratory section of this site.
