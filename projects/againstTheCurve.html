What I Built

Against The Curve is my take on the classic asteroid-style arcade game. You survive waves of incoming asteroids and hostile saucers, and the saucers shoot back, so every encounter is a test of reflexes and precision. The difficulty keeps rising with no cap, so the question is how long you can last.
