Overview

  • Role: Solo developer.
  • Engine: Unity 6 with its new VR system.
  • Duration: a two to three day personal challenge.
  • Platforms: Windows and Android (standalone VR).

What I Built

Bear Catacombs was a personal challenge: make a complete, looping VR game in two to three days, with polish and cohesion rather than just a rough prototype. You move through a linear, rail-style level and shoot enemies as they appear, earning points for each one. At the end of a run you see your score, which is there to make you want another go.

I made some of the assets myself and used others from the Fab Store and the Unity Asset Store, so that the time could go into getting the game to feel finished.
