Overview

  • Genre: stealth heist.
  • Team: five people.
  • Platform: Windows.
  • Status: prototype.

The Game

Robbeary is a stealth game set in a museum guarded by robots. The goal is to steal a bear statue that everyone insists is perfectly ordinary, and to get out before the world ends. Robot guards patrol day and night and cameras cover the rooms, so you have to watch their routes and move when it's safe.

Controls

  • Grapple: left mouse button.
  • Dark Vision: right mouse button.
  • Jump: Space.
  • Interact: E.
  • Crouch: Ctrl.
